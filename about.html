About us

A place in
Fort Washakie’s
history.

Wind River Trading Company is part of the property where Hines General Store began. Its buildings hold a history of everyday trade, family work, and community connections that continues today.

The property through the years

The general store came first.

Groceries, fuel, supplies, and tribal fishing permits made this a practical stop for local families.

1946

Paul and Peg Hines

Paul and Marjorie “Peg” Hines purchased the general store after Paul returned from World War II. Peg had come to Fort Washakie to work as a field nurse.

1963

Don and Sharon Hines

Don and Sharon took over from Paul and Peg, expanding the family business and continuing to serve the surrounding community.

1978

The property expands

A major building project brought a new general store and changes to the Sacajawea Service Station. The former grocery space eventually became the Wind River Trading Company gift shop.

2004

Dave and Ben Hines

After years working alongside their parents, brothers Dave and Ben took over operations, carrying the family business into its third generation.

Today

Shared roots, daily work

Groceries next door. Local beadwork and art here. The businesses continue to serve Fort Washakie and the Wind River Reservation.

Beyond the storefront

A place in
community life.

Don Hines’s involvement included the Wind River Indian Athletic Association, the Ethete powwow and rodeo committees, and sponsorship of reservation events.

That history is part of what you visit today: a working store and the Gallery of the Wind, with handmade pieces, photographs, and displays to spend time with.
