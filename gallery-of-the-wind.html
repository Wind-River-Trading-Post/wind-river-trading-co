Gallery of the Wind

A different kind of discovery

The stories beyond
the sales floor.

Eastern Shoshone and Northern Arapaho artwork, photographs, beadwork, and older objects fill the Gallery of the Wind. These are displays to explore in person, gathered inside a working trading post.

Local history

Chief Washakie’s story.

A wall display recounts the 1866 Crowheart Butte story and the Eastern Shoshone leader whose name Fort Washakie carries.

Look a little closer

Details worth your time.

Notice the bead colors, stitching, and worn materials in the moccasins and handmade pieces, alongside historic photographs and case displays.
